AponiaBot

AponiaBot is the web crawler of Aponia Analytics. It collects publicly available regulatory, health-technology-assessment and clinical-trial information for our research on the biomedical sector.

How it identifies itself

Every request carries this User-Agent:

AponiaBot/1.0 (Aponia Analytics; +https://aponiaanalytics.com/bot; bot@aponiaanalytics.com)

How it behaves

  • It reads and obeys robots.txt before fetching from a site.
  • It spaces its requests to each site and keeps its overall request rate low.
  • It fetches publicly available pages and published data only.

How to block or limit it

Add these lines to your site's robots.txt:

User-agent: AponiaBot
Disallow: /

Contact

Questions or concerns: bot@aponiaanalytics.com. We read and answer this address.

Independence
